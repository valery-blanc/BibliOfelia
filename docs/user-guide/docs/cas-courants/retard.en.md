# Long overdue

When a member is far past the return date of a book, you have
several levers depending on the severity.

## Track overdues

The dashboard permanently shows the **Overdue loans** counter and
the **Follow-ups** section at the bottom, listing the most overdue
loans.

For a complete view, open **Reports → Overdue loans**.

## First level: call the member

For 1- to 2-week overdues:

1. Open the member's profile from the overdue list
2. Note their phone (visible in the Contact section)
3. Call them to remind about the return

Ideally, make a call round each week.

## Second level: block new loans

While a member has an overdue loan, BibliOfelia keeps letting them
borrow (unless you change settings). If you want to block new loans
when overdue:

- Simple solution: **deactivate** the member temporarily from their
  profile. Reactivate when they bring the books back.
- Conventional solution: agree on an informal rule ("no loan if
  overdue") and apply it manually at the desk.

## Third level: mark the book as lost

For very long overdues (several months) where you consider the book
definitively lost:

1. Open the member's profile
2. Find the affected loan
3. Click **Lost**

See [Lost or damaged book](livre-perdu.md) for consequences.

## Re-enrolling an old member

If a member returns to the library after a long absence with a book
overdue for years:

- Accept the return normally (no blocking at return)
- Renew the card if expired
- Discuss the lost book (you may decide to absolve them)

## Communicating with members

BibliOfelia does not automatically generate reminder letters. A few
options depending on your means:

- **Phone**: most effective for a small library
- **SMS** from your own phone (BibliOfelia does not do it)
- **Display**: list of overdues on a board at the entrance
- **Word of mouth**: in small communities, it works
