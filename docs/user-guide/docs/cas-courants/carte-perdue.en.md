# Lost or stolen card

When a member reports a lost or stolen card, you must assign a **new
card number** (not reprint the old one) to invalidate the lost card.

## Why a new number?

If the old card is found by someone else, that person could use it
to borrow books on behalf of the original member (for example, a
child picking up a neighbor's card).

By assigning a new number, the old card no longer works at all: any
scan would return an error.

## The procedure

1. Open the [member profile](../usagers/fiche.md)
2. Click **Replace card**
3. Confirm

BibliOfelia:

- Assigns a new card number
- Retires the old number for good: it will never be reassigned to
  another member
- Keeps the entire member history (loans, reservations, enrollments)

Then print the new card normally (see
[Print cards](../impressions/cartes.md)).

## While waiting for the new card

The member can keep borrowing without the physical card:

- On the **Lend** page, look up the member by name in the global
  search
- Identify them on their profile, then register the loan normally

## If the card is found later

If the member finds the old card after receiving the new one,
**destroy the old card**: it no longer works, and risks creating
confusion.

!!! info "Why not reuse the old number?"
    All printed codes (cards and book labels) are single-use in
    BibliOfelia: once retired, they are never reassigned. This rule
    prevents an old card lying around from suddenly borrowing in
    someone else's name by mistake.
