# Print member cards

Member cards are generated as PDFs from BibliOfelia. You can print
one or several at a time.

## Open the printing page

**Advanced → Printing → Cards**.

![Card selection page](../assets/screenshots/en/impressions/cards-picker.png)

## Choose the cards to print

The list shows all members. You can:

- **Filter** by category, enrollment date, status
- **Tick** the relevant members (checkbox at the start of the row)
- **Tick all** from the header to print all filtered cards at once

## Choose the language

The **Language** selector at the top sets the language shown on the
card (labels "Member card", "Expires on", etc.). Handy to print
cards in French for francophones and in Malagasy for others.

## Generate the PDF

Click **Generate PDF**. A new tab opens with the PDF ready to print.
Each page contains several cards at business card size (85 × 54 mm).

## What a card contains

- OFELIA logo as watermark (cream background)
- Member photo (top left, if provided)
- Last name and first name in large font
- Card number and EAN-13 barcode
- Expiration date
- Flag of the preferred language

## Printing tips

- **200-300 g/m²** paper for a sturdy card
- **Laminate** afterwards to make it water- and fold-resistant
- You can also print on **sticky labels** to attach to pre-printed
  blank cards

!!! tip "Print in series for new enrollments"
    If you enroll several members the same day, batch them and print
    in lot in the evening: less paper waste (cards are arranged 6 per
    A4 page).
