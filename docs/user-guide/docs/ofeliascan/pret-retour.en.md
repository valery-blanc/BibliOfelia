# Loan and return with OfeliaScan

OfeliaScan does not lend and return on its own: it only **scans**
barcodes. BibliOfelia on the computer is what records the operation.

The principle: your phone is a "wireless scanner".

## Typical workflow for a loan

1. On the computer, open the **Lend** page
2. Click **Scan the card** in the Member section
3. OfeliaScan opens on your phone (vibration or sound)
4. Point the camera at the member's card
5. As soon as the code is read, the phone vibrates — BibliOfelia
   identifies the member automatically
6. Click **Scan a book** in the Books section
7. Scan each book one after the other
8. Click **Lend N books** to confirm

## Typical workflow for a return

Even simpler:

1. Open the **Return** page
2. Click **Scan a book**
3. Scan all returned books, one after another
4. Each scan automatically records the return

## When to prefer OfeliaScan over the scanner?

- **The workstation is far from the shelf** where the books are
- **Several aisles** to walk through
- **Crate of books** on the floor, crouched, scanner not practical
- **More than one person** serving in parallel (several phones, one
  central station)

## When the scanner remains better

- **Immediate reception** (members filing in at the desk): the wired
  scanner is faster
- **Damaged barcodes**: OfeliaScan can struggle with bent or
  scratched labels that the laser scanner reads without trouble

## Common problems

### OfeliaScan won't open

Check that:

- The phone is paired with the Box (see [Activate OfeliaScan](activer.md))
- The phone and the Box are on the same Wi-Fi network
- The app isn't put in deep sleep by Android (exclude OfeliaScan from
  battery deep sleep in the Android settings)

### Scanning does not trigger anything

The barcode may be poorly read: try moving the phone away or closer,
check the lighting. You can also type the code on the keyboard
directly on the computer.
