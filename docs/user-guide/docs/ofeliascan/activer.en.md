# Activate OfeliaScan

**OfeliaScan** is the Android companion app for BibliOfelia. Once
installed and paired with your Ofelia Box, it lets you scan barcodes
from a phone and send the result directly to BibliOfelia on the
computer.

## When to use it?

- **Inventory**: walking the stacks without pushing a workstation
  with a scanner
- **Cataloging**: registering books in series from an arrival crate
- **When your scanner is broken**: OfeliaScan saves the day with a
  smartphone

## Install OfeliaScan

The app installs from:

- The Android **Play Store** (search for "OfeliaScan")
- Or by **direct download** from your Ofelia Box if you don't have the
  Play Store

Ask the Box administrator for the exact installation procedure.

## Pair the phone

Once OfeliaScan is installed, you need to connect it to your Box
(to do **once only**).

1. On the computer, open BibliOfelia and go to **Advanced → OfeliaScan
   access**
2. A QR code is displayed on screen
3. On the phone, open OfeliaScan and tap **Pair**
4. Point the camera at the QR code
5. Pairing is confirmed on both screens

The phone is now linked to your Box. It will remember on each use.

!!! info "Several phones, one Box"
    Several librarians can pair their phone to the same Box. Each
    sees their own scans in BibliOfelia.

## Use the Scanner banner

On the **dashboard** and on the **Lend** / **Return** pages, a
**Scan a card or a book** banner directly opens OfeliaScan on your
phone:

1. Click the banner in BibliOfelia (on the PC)
2. OfeliaScan opens on your phone
3. Scan the barcode
4. BibliOfelia automatically switches to the right page

This is the fastest way to chain mobile scanning and on-station
action.
