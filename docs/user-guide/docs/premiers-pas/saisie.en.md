# Input methods: scanner, OfeliaScan or keyboard?

BibliOfelia is designed to work with or without specific hardware.
Choose the mode that fits your equipment.

## The barcode scanner (recommended for daily use)

A USB barcode scanner plugged into your workstation behaves like a
keyboard: it types the barcode contents and presses **Enter**
automatically. This is the fastest, least tiring mode.

**Advantages:**

- Instant action (less than 1 second per scan)
- No hand fatigue
- Works on any page with an input field

**Limitation:** the scanner must be plugged into the workstation
you're using.

## OfeliaScan (mobile phone)

OfeliaScan is the Android companion app for BibliOfelia. You scan with
your phone camera, and the result is sent to BibliOfelia.

**Advantages:**

- No need for a scanner or workstation next to every shelf
- Ideal for inventory (walking through the stacks)

**Limitations:**

- First-time setup: you must [pair the phone](../ofeliascan/activer.md)
- Slower than a scanner (the camera has to read the code)

## The keyboard (always available)

On every page with an input field, you can type the code manually and
press **Enter** or click the **Validate** button next to it.

**When to use it:**

- No scanner connected
- The barcode is damaged or poorly printed
- You need to type a code you wrote down by hand

!!! tip "Tip"
    On the **Lend** and **Return** pages, the cursor automatically
    moves to the right field after each scan or validation. You never
    need to click manually to move to the next book.

## Searching by name instead of barcode

If you don't have the member's card or the book in hand, use the
**global search** at the top of every page: type the member's name or
a word from the title, and BibliOfelia takes you directly to the right
page.
