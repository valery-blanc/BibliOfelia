# Renewal and expiration

Each member card has a validity period (1 year by default). On
expiration, the member can no longer borrow until the card is renewed.

## See cards about to expire

The **dashboard** shows a counter of cards approaching expiration.
Click it to see the list of affected members.

You can also filter the member list by expiration date from
**Members → Filters**.

## Renew a card

From the [member profile](fiche.md), click **Renew card**.

The expiration date is pushed back by a standard duration (1 year by
default) starting today. The card number does not change — the member
can keep using their physical card without a new print.

!!! tip "Early renewal"
    You can renew a card before it expires. The new expiration date is
    calculated from today, **not** from the previous date — meaning
    you do not lose the unused days.

## Expired card: what happens?

When a member arrives with an expired card:

- **At loan**: a red alert appears. You cannot finalize the loan
  until the card is renewed.
- **At return**: no blocking. Return is always accepted, even with an
  expired card (otherwise overdue books would stay in the library
  forever).

## Renew while chatting with the member

Take advantage of renewal to check:

- Contact info still up to date (phone, address, email)
- Recent photo (especially for children growing fast)
- Suitable category (a child who became a teen should change category
  for adapted loan durations)

## Deactivate rather than letting expire

If a member no longer comes to the library, prefer **Deactivate** the
profile (from the Actions menu) rather than waiting for expiration.
This avoids including them in your active member statistics.
