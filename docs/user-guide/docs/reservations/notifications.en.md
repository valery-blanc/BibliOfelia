# Notifications and follow-ups

BibliOfelia works offline and does not send automatic emails or SMS.
**It is up to you to call members** when a reserved book arrives.
This page explains how to track notifications to do.

## The Notifications to do card

On the [dashboard](../premiers-pas/dashboard.md), between the tiles
and the scanner banner, a **Notifications to do** card displays
reservations whose book has arrived but you have not yet notified the
member.

Each row shows:

- The **book** ready for pickup
- The **member** to notify
- The **phone** or **email** of the member (if recorded)
- The **date** the book arrived

## Mark a notification as done

After calling the member:

1. Click the notification row
2. Tick **Member notified** or click **I notified**
3. The reservation disappears from the Notifications card

From that moment, the **pickup** countdown begins (see
[Pickup and expiration](retrait.md)).

## Follow up on old notifications

If you notified a member a few days ago and they still have not come
to pick up their book, you can:

- See the reservation history in the **Reservations** page
- Filter by status **Ready for pickup** + date to see reservations
  lingering
- Call again to follow up

## For libraries without a phone

If you can't reach members directly (no phone, limited internet),
display ready reservations on a paper board or a sign at the library
entrance. Members will check themselves when passing by.

!!! tip "Organization tip"
    Print the list of ready reservations each week and pin it near
    the welcome desk: it's your **call list** for the week.
